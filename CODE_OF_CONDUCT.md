# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant], in full.
The canonical text lives at that link and governs participation here — in
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## In short

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Accept that people have different levels of
experience with Ansible, Windows, and Kubernetes, and that a question which
seems obvious to you may not be.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to the maintainer, [@ka1ne][maintainer], by
direct message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter
is not identified to the person reported without their agreement.

[maintainer]: https://github.com/ka1ne

Maintainers who do not act in good faith on a report may face consequences
determined by other members of the project's leadership.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows the Community Impact Guidelines set out in the Contributor
Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban, escalating with
the severity and persistence of the behaviour.
